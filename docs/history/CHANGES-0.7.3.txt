MinerDesk 0.7.3

System tray mining status
- The Windows tray menu now shows live mining information from the privileged backend.
- One active miner: profile, hashrate, power, temperature/fan, accepted/rejected shares and uptime.
- Several active miners: active count, total power, up to two profile/hashrate summaries, maximum temperature and aggregate shares.
- No active miner: clearly shows that mining is stopped.
- Backend unavailable: the tray reports that status instead of showing stale values.
- Information refreshes every 4 seconds while the desktop process is running.
- No additional GPU tuning or mining limits are introduced.
